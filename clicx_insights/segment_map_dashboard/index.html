How customers are classified
Borrow
Has ever booked a loan with CLICX.
Pay
Paid in-app in October 2026 (Bill Pay / Top Up or Scan and Pay).
Core saver
Balance over ฿20K. The Save Max/More flag is not used.
Emerging saver
Balance ฿101–20K.
Active
Borrows, pays or saves (balance over ฿100). Anyone who has booked a loan sits with the borrowers, whatever their balance.
Region
